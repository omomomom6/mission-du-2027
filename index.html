How to use your planner

The chapter study method

  1. Watch the chapter-wise one-shot for understanding.
  2. Read the corresponding NCERT content.
  3. Make concise notes (2–4 pages where appropriate).
  4. Solve 20–30 MCQs and numerical examples as relevant.
  5. Attempt 15–20 questions without notes.
  6. Revise after 1, 7 and 30 days.

For every two hours of lectures, aim for at least one hour of active practice. Weekly revision days are catch-up buffers; don't sacrifice understanding to tick a box.

Daily non-negotiables

Complete the dated subjects, read NCERT, practise 40–60 mixed MCQs, solve Accountancy numericals independently, practise English 45–60 minutes, revise yesterday's work, update the mistake notebook, and sleep adequately.
